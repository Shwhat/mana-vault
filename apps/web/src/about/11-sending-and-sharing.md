# Sending and sharing

Collections and decks belong to the account that made them. You can send one to another account on the same server as a copy, as a share, or as a transfer. Nothing changes for the other person until they accept.

## Ways to send

| Way | What happens when they accept | Who can send it this way |
|---|---|---|
| Copy | They get their own copy. Yours is unchanged, and later changes to either copy do not affect the other | The owner, or anyone it is shared with |
| Share | You both see and edit the same collection or deck | The owner only |
| Transfer | It moves to their account and leaves yours. Anyone it was shared with loses it | The owner only |

## Sending

1. Open the collection (collection view) or deck.
2. Choose **Send collection...** or **Send deck...** (the Share group on the Collection or Decks ribbon tab).
3. Pick the account to send to from the list of this server's accounts, and pick Copy, Share or Transfer.
4. Choose **Send**. The message "Sent to name. It is added when they accept it." confirms it.

The offer waits in their Inbox. Only one offer of the same item to the same person can wait at a time.

## The Inbox

A count on your account menu (and on the phone's top bar) shows offers waiting for you. **Inbox...** in the account menu opens it. It has two lists:

- **Waiting for you**: each offer with its name, kind, way and sender, and **Accept...** or **Decline**.
- **Sent by you, not yet answered**: your waiting offers, each with **Cancel offer**.

The count refreshes every minute and whenever the Inbox opens.

## Accepting

The sender's choice of Copy, Share or Transfer stays as sent. **Accept...** lets you choose how it enters your library:

| Offer | Your choices |
|---|---|
| Collection, Copy or Transfer | Add it as a new collection under a name you choose (the sender's name by default), or merge its cards into one of your collections. Merging adds its counts to that collection; a transferred collection is then removed from the sender |
| Deck, Copy or Transfer | Add it under a name you choose |
| Share | Give it your own name. The shared item keeps one real name for its owner; the name you choose is shown only to you |

A copied or transferred collection keeps its description, rules and linked sets. If the item was deleted, or the sender lost the right to send it, accepting shows "This item is no longer available to send".

**Decline** removes the offer without changing anything.

## Shared collections and decks

- Both people can add, change, move and remove cards, link sets and change the settings.
- Only the owner can delete it, send it as a share or transfer, or stop sharing it.
- Rows in the Collections and Decks panels show a **Shared** badge. The collection and deck views show "Shared by name" or "Shared with names".
- **Collection sharing...** and **Deck sharing...** list the people an item you own is shared with, each with **Stop sharing**.
- The other person can stop using it with **Leave shared collection...** or **Leave shared deck...** (these replace Delete for shared items).
- Deleting a shared item you own warns that the other people will lose it, and removes it for everyone.
